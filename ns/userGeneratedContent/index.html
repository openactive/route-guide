userGeneratedContent

Links to user-generated content, such as e.g. images or descriptions of the Route, video or path-recordings of their traversal of the Route, etc. If this field is populated, it is recommended that the data-publisher have some means of filtering and otherwise curating user-generated content prior to publication.

This property can be used on: rt:RouteGuide

This property may include the values: schema:CreativeWork
